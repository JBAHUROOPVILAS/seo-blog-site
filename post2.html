Keyword Research Strategy

Keyword research is the process of identifying what users are searching for online. Choosing the wrong keywords means your content will never be found.

Types of Keywords

  • Short-tail: Broad and competitive
  • Long-tail: Specific and easier to rank

How to Find Keywords

Use tools like Google Keyword Planner, Ubersuggest, or Ahrefs. Look for keywords with good search volume and low competition.

Common Mistakes

  • Targeting very competitive keywords
  • Ignoring user intent
