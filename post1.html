SEO Basics for Beginners

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) is the foundation of digital visibility. If your website is not optimized, it will not appear in search results even if your content is useful.

What is SEO?

SEO is the process of improving a website’s visibility in search engines like Google. It helps search engines understand your content and rank it based on relevance.

How Search Engines Work

Search engines follow three steps:

  • Crawling: Bots scan your website
  • Indexing: Content is stored in database
  • Ranking: Pages are ordered based on quality

Key Ranking Factors

  • Relevant keywords
  • High-quality content
  • Mobile-friendly design
  • Website speed

Why SEO Matters

Without SEO, your content is invisible. SEO helps bring organic traffic, which is more sustainable than paid ads.
